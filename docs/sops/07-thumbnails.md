# Thumbnail SOP

**Made For**
Members publishing long-form YouTube videos who need a clear, on-brand thumbnail without a designer.

**SOP Intention**
A viewer understands what the video is about within one second of seeing the thumbnail on a phone, and the thumbnail promises exactly what the video delivers.

**Process Summary**
The kit pulls frames from your original recording. Apple Vision picks the frame with the biggest clear face. The kit then lays out 2–4 words in your font and accent colour on the side away from your face, and checks the result:
- text inside the frame;
- face visible;
- text off the face;
- text detectable at phone size.

You compare 2–3 word options and pick one.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Choose The Words (The Hard Part)
- **2–4 words.** Not the title repeated, and no metaphors the viewer has to decode.
- State the topic or the result plainly: "FIRST CLIENT IN 9 DAYS", "STOP UNDERCHARGING".
- Only numbers that are true and said in the video.

### Step 2: Make It
> "Make a thumbnail for L003 that says FIRST CLIENT IN 9 DAYS."

The output is `output/L003/thumbnail/thumbnail.png`, plus `thumbnail-phone.png`, which shows what the feed shows.

### Step 3: Judge It At Phone Size
Open `thumbnail-phone.png`. If you can't read it in one second, use fewer words.

### Step 4: Make A Second Option
> "Make another one that says STOP CHASING CLIENTS, as option B."

YouTube's "Test & Compare" can run both.

### Step 5: Want A Different Frame Or Pose?
Record 5 seconds of a deliberate expression (pointing, surprised, holding a prop) at the end of your recording. The picker favours big, clear faces.

### Practical Example
- **Title:** "How I Landed My First Client Without Case Studies"
- **Thumbnail:** "NO CASE STUDIES?" in white, "CASE STUDIES" in your accent colour, your face on the left.

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- 4 words or fewer, readable on the phone preview.
- All text inside the frame; QA shows `text_inside_frame` PASS.
- Face clear and not covered.
- The promise matches the video.
- No logos or images you don't have the right to use.
