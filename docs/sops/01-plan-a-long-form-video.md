# Plan A Long-Form Video SOP

**Made For**
Members who want to record a long-form YouTube video (8–15 minutes) that a prospect could watch before a sales call, and who want to record it in one take from talking points rather than a script.

**SOP Intention**
Walk into the recording with a hook you can say word-for-word and talking points you can riff on, so the video lands one clear promise, handles the viewer's objections and ends on your call to action.

**Process Summary**
Pick a topic that comes from a real question your audience asks. Claude writes a riff guide: a scripted hook, 3–5 sections of bullet points with one proof point each, re-hook lines between sections and a scripted CTA. It also gives title and thumbnail options. You record from bullets, not a teleprompter, so you sound like yourself.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Choose The Topic From A Real Question
Use questions from comments, DMs, sales calls or your community: "How do I get my first client without a portfolio?" beats "5 tips for growth". One video answers one question.

### Step 2: Ask Claude For The Plan
> "Plan a long-form video about how to get your first client without case studies. The story I want to use: my first client came from a referral in 9 days."

Claude saves `projects/<topic>/plan.md`.

### Step 3: Check The Hook
- Said in the first 8 seconds, at most 20 words, no "hey guys" or "in this video".
- It states the outcome or the problem, not your name.
- Pick one of the 3 options (or ask for more).

### Step 4: Check The Sections
- 3–5 sections, each with 2–4 bullets and one example, number or story. All of these must be true and yours.
- Teach what to do and why it works. Your paid programme is the full how, so don't give away every step.
- Each section ends on a re-hook line that pulls into the next.

### Step 5: Lock The Call To Action
It is your CTA from onboarding, in your words, scripted. Say it once, clearly, near the end.

### Step 6: Pick Title And Thumbnail Text Now
Choosing them before recording keeps the video on-promise. Titles are under 60 characters. Thumbnail text is 2–4 words that don't repeat the title.

### Practical Example
- **Hook:** "Nobody needs case studies to land a first client. They need proof you can do the job. Here's how to show it."
- **Section 2 bullets:** do one small piece of free work for a warm lead · show the result, not the hours · *proof:* "my first client came from exactly this in 9 days"
- **Re-hook:** "But the free work is worthless if you pitch it wrong."

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- One question answered, and it came from a real audience source.
- The hook is 20 words or fewer, with zero preamble.
- Every number or result in the plan is real and yours.
- Each section has one proof point.
- The CTA matches your onboarding profile exactly.
- A title and 2–4 words of thumbnail text are chosen.
