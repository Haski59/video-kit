# Shorts From Long-Form SOP

**Made For**
Members who have recorded a long-form video and want 3–5 vertical shorts from it for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and LinkedIn.

**SOP Intention**
Get several stand-alone shorts from every recording. Each short carries one complete point (hook → why → proof → payoff), so it works for someone who never saw the long video and pulls them toward your call to action.

**Process Summary**
The kit transcribes the recording once and splits it into sentences. Claude then picks the strongest 60–120-second runs that open on a clean, strong line and finish their point. If Claude isn't available, a rule-based picker is used instead. Each clip is cut as its own vertical short (S001, S002…), with:
- face-tracked 9:16 framing;
- captions;
- emphasis on key words;
- its own QA.

Thirty-second fragments that stop before the point lands are rejected by design.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Ask For Clips
> "Make 3 shorts from this."

You can use the original recording or say "from L003". Shorter or longer: "make 4 shorts, 45 to 90 seconds".

### Step 2: Check Each Clip's First And Last Line
Claude lists each short with its opening line. A good opener makes sense with no context ("Nobody needs case studies to land a first client."). A bad one starts with "And so…" or "Like I said…". Ask Claude to re-cut a weak one a sentence earlier or later.

### Step 3: Watch Each Short Once
On your phone, check:
- the face stays in frame;
- captions are readable and off your face;
- the last line lands the point.

### Step 4: Pick A Specific Moment Yourself (Optional)
> "Make a short from 3:20 to 4:45."

### Step 5: Get The Post Captions
> "Write the captions for these shorts."

That gives one caption per platform (SOP 08).

### Practical Example
> **Claude:** 3 shorts:
> - S004 ✅ 1:18 "Nobody needs case studies to land a first client."
> - S005 ✅ 1:32 "The free-work trick only works if you pitch it like this."
> - S006 ⚠️ 1:05 "Price is never the real objection." Listen at 0:41 for a possible double take.

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- Each short opens on a line that makes sense on its own.
- Each short ends after the point is made, not mid-list.
- The face is in frame throughout, and captions are clear of it.
- Any flagged time has been listened to.
- There's no "as I said earlier" or reference to the long video's other parts.
- Length is 60–120 s, unless you chose otherwise.
