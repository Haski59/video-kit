# Standalone Short-Form SOP

**Made For**
Members who record shorts on purpose (vertical, 60–120 s, phone or camera) rather than cutting them from a long video.

**SOP Intention**
Publish a polished vertical short within minutes of recording it: tight pacing, captions, emphasis on the key line, safe from platform buttons and overlays.

**Process Summary**
Plan the short as one point (claim → why → proof → payoff), record it vertically, and hand it to the kit. The edit:
- removes dead air, fillers and retakes inside real pauses;
- adds a push-in on the hook and at most a couple of callouts;
- places captions below your face, inside the area platform buttons don't cover;
- checks loudness, sync and framing.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Plan In One Breath
> "Plan a short about why cheap clients cost more."

You get a scripted hook and 3–4 bullets in the claim → why → proof → payoff order.

### Step 2: Record Vertically
Follow SOP 02 with the camera vertical (9:16). Keep your face in the upper-middle of the frame and leave the bottom third clear for captions.

### Step 3: Edit
Drag the file into Claude Code:
> "Edit this short."

The output is `output/S###/renders/v1.mp4` (1080×1920) plus an `.srt`.

### Step 4: Check On Your Phone
AirDrop the file to your phone and play it full-screen:
- the hook is said within 3 seconds;
- captions aren't hidden by the like and comment buttons;
- the last line lands.

### Step 5: Post With A Platform Caption
> "Write the captions for S007."

### Practical Example
- **Hook:** "Your cheapest client is costing you the most money."
- **Why:** they take the most time.
- **Proof:** "my $500 client used 3x the hours of my $3k client."
- **Payoff:** "Raise your floor, not your workload."

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- One point, with the hook in the first 3 seconds.
- 60–120 s long, ending on the payoff line.
- Captions readable on a phone and not under the platform buttons.
- No claims beyond what you said and can back up.
- A platform caption written for each place you post.
