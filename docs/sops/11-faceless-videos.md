# Faceless Videos SOP

**Made For**
Members who want to publish more often than they can film, or who want explainer videos without being on camera: shorts and short long-form built from an idea or a script.

**SOP Intention**
Turn an idea into a finished, on-brand video, with an AI voice-over, motion graphics and captions, without filming. Every word is something you approved, and the video passes the same QA as your edited videos.

**Process Summary**
1. Claude writes the script in your voice. You approve it.
2. A local AI voice (Kokoro, free, runs on your Mac) reads it.
3. HyperFrames builds the visuals (typography, diagrams, shapes and captions) in your brand. If you give a reference, it follows that video's style grammar: shot length, how text moves, caption look.
4. The kit's QA checks the render: format, sound, sync, and that every line of the script is actually spoken.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Give The Idea (And Optionally A Reference)
> "Make a faceless short about why cheap clients cost more, in the style of this: <link>"

Without a reference, it uses a clean default in your brand.

### Step 2: Approve The Script
Read it out loud once. Change anything that doesn't sound like you. Every number and claim must be yours. Rendering starts only after you say OK.

### Step 3: Choose The Voice
There are several English voices, male and female, US and UK. Say "British male voice" or "female, warm". The speed follows the reference's pace.

### Step 4: Let It Render
A 60-second short takes a few minutes on an M-series Mac. Claude checks it with `kit qa` and looks at frames before telling you it's done.

### Step 5: Thumbnail And Publish Pack
> "Make a thumbnail and the publish pack for it."

### Practical Example
- **Script hook:** "Your cheapest client is costing you the most money."
- **The video:** bold kinetic text of the hook, a three-step diagram for the why, the "$500 vs $3k client" comparison as a bar graphic for the proof, your CTA card at the end, 2-word captions throughout. 58 seconds, QA COMPLETE.

### Upgrades (Optional, Paid, Your Choice)
- **HyperFrames Pro:** proven series templates (`style-sampling`) and frame-by-frame rebuilds of a video you have the rights to (`reverse-any-video`). Presenter re-shoots use HeyGen credits.
- **Your own cloned voice:** VoiceStudio (check its model licence for commercial use).

Claude never starts a paid step without your OK.

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- You approved the script, and every claim in it is yours.
- QA is COMPLETE, including "script heard in order".
- No other creator's footage, face, logo or music is used.
- Captions are readable, and nothing overflows the frame (Claude checked frames).
- Your CTA is in the video and in the description.
