# Recording SOP

**Made For**
Members recording talking-head videos on a camera or phone, at home or in a small studio, who will hand the file straight to the kit.

**SOP Intention**
Record footage the kit can edit cleanly: clear audio, a well-framed face, and natural pauses between thoughts, so the automatic edit needs no fixing.

**Process Summary**
The kit cuts dead air, filler words and retakes by listening to the audio and finding real pauses. It also frames shorts by tracking your face. Good audio and steady framing matter more than an expensive camera. Pause, then restart a sentence when you slip: the kit spots repeated takes.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Audio First
- Use a microphone: a lav clipped to your chest, or a USB/shotgun mic within arm's length. The phone's built-in mic in a quiet room is the minimum.
- Choose a quiet room: soft furnishings, no fan or air-con hum, and windows closed.
- Record 3 seconds of silence at the start. It helps the kit measure the room.

### Step 2: Frame For Both Formats
- Landscape (16:9) for long-form. Keep your head and shoulders in the **middle third** with some space above your head. Shorts are cropped from the centre, so don't sit at the edge of the frame.
- Camera at eye level, about an arm and a half away.
- Light in front of you (a window or a soft light), not behind you.

### Step 3: Settings
- 1080p or 4K, at 25 or 30 fps. Keep the frame rate the same for the whole video.
- Shorts-only recording: shoot vertical (9:16) and use the same framing rules.

### Step 4: How To Talk
- Open with the scripted hook from your plan. Then talk through your bullet points.
- **Made a mistake? Stop, breathe (one second of silence), and say the sentence again from its start.** The kit finds the repeat and keeps the good take. If it isn't sure, it flags it for you.
- Pause between sections. The kit tightens long pauses to a natural beat, but it never cuts inside a word.
- Avoid saying "as I said earlier" in parts you want as shorts: each short has to stand alone.

### Step 5: Hand Over The File
Copy the file to your Mac (AirDrop, cable or Drive). Open Video Kit, drag the file onto the frame on **Edit a recording** and click **Make my videos** (SOP 03). Prefer Claude Code? Drag it into the `Video Kit` folder's Claude Code session and say "edit this".

### Practical Example
You stumble on "the three things you need to get clients is…". Stop. Pause. Say "The three things you need to get clients are…" cleanly. The kit removes the first attempt. If the two takes differ a lot, it leaves both in and tells you the second to check.

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- Test clip played back with headphones: voice clear, no hum, no clipping.
- Face centred, eyes about a third from the top, with headroom.
- Light on your face, not behind you.
- Same frame rate for the whole recording, 1080p or higher.
- Hook delivered word-for-word in the first 8 seconds.
- Retakes done as pause → full sentence again.
