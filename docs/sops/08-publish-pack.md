# Publish Pack SOP

**Made For**
Members about to upload an edited long-form video and its shorts, who want the title, description, chapters, tags and per-platform captions written in their voice.

**SOP Intention**
Make the words around the video continue the promise the video makes. The description leads with your one call to action, chapters match the real edit, and nothing claims more than you said.

**Process Summary**
Claude reads the captions file of the finished edit (so chapter times match the video you are uploading), your plan if you made one, and your profile. It then writes:
- 3 titles;
- a description with your CTA on line 1 and chapters;
- tags and a pinned comment;
- one caption per short per platform.

Every claim is checked against the transcript and your profile. The text is passed through the humanizer so it doesn't read as AI-written.

## The Steps Broken Down

### Step 1: Ask For The Pack
> "Write the publish pack for L003 and its shorts."

It's saved to `output/L003/publish-pack.md`.

### Step 2: Check Line 1 Of The Description
It must be your CTA and link, exactly as in your profile. This is what shows above "…more".

### Step 3: Check The Chapters
Open the video at two chapter times and confirm they start where the label says. Chapters start at 00:00, there are at least 3, and they are at least 10 seconds apart.

### Step 4: Pick A Title
It should match the thumbnail but not repeat it, be under 60 characters, and promise something the video delivers in the first minute.

### Step 5: Post (You Do This, Not The Kit)
Copy each part into YouTube Studio, TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn. Upload the `.srt` as YouTube subtitles. The kit never posts for you.

### Practical Example
- **Line 1:** "Book a free strategy call with my team 👉 yourlink.com/call"
- **Chapters:** "00:00 You don't need case studies · 01:12 The free-work trick · 04:30 How to pitch it · 08:05 What to charge next"

## Pre-Launch Checklist
- Description line 1 is your CTA, with the right link.
- Chapters match the actual edit (two spot-checked).
- The title is under 60 characters and delivered by the video.
- No invented numbers, results or testimonials.
- None of your never-say words appear.
- A caption is written for each platform you post to.
- The `.srt` is uploaded to YouTube.
