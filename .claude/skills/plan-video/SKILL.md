# /plan-video — a riff guide the member can record from in one take

Read `member/voice-and-offer.md` first. If it's missing, run `/setup`.

## Ask (one message)
- The topic, and the one question a viewer has that this video answers.
- Long-form (8–15 min) or a short (60–120 s)?
- Any story, client result or example they want to use. Only use proof they give you or that is in their profile.

## Write `projects/<short-slug>/plan.md`
1. **Angle** — one sentence: who it's for, and the promise.
2. **Hook (scripted, ≤ 20 words, said in the first 8 seconds)** — no preamble ("hey guys", "in this video"). Give 3 options and mark your pick.
3. **Talking points** — 3–5 sections. Each: a heading, 2–4 bullets of what to say (not full sentences), and one example or proof point. Short-form: 1 section, structured claim → why → proof → resolution.
4. **Re-hook lines** — one line between sections that pulls the viewer forward ("But that's not the expensive mistake…").
5. **Call to action (scripted)** — their exact CTA from the profile, said in their voice.
6. **Titles** — 3 options, under 60 characters, specific, no clickbait the video doesn't deliver.
7. **Thumbnail text** — 3 options, 2–4 words each, readable in 1 second, not repeating the title.
8. **Recording checklist** — point them to SOP 02.

Use the `copywriting` skill from marketing-skills for the hook and titles if it is installed, then pass the whole plan through the `humanizer` skill. Keep their tone. No invented numbers.

Finish by showing the hook + section headings in the chat and the path to `plan.md`.
